## Exclude punchy (snippet)
`mumbled soft washed vocals, buried vocals behind the beat, excessive wet reverb on lead, sleepy legato only`

## Edit NL V6 types
- Groove : `Tighten kick-snare pocket, more punchy dry kick, keep swing hats, no new drums rewrite`
- Voix : `Make lead vocals more punchy and percussive, hard consonant attack, dry front-of-mix presence, less wet reverb, Cel-B answers louder after every hook line, keep the same melody and lyrics`
- Bridge / Pont : `Strip half-bar then explode stacked harmonies on the bridge peak, more energy 2:14-2:40, keep the same lyrics`
- C&R : `Stronger call-and-response every line, male lead then immediate female high tenor answer`
- Chaîne recommandée club/urban : **Groove → Voix → Pont**
